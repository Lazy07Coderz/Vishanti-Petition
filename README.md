**The Vishanti Petition — Campus Grievance Redressal System**

The Vishanti Petition is a web-based grievance management platform developed by **Team LazyCoderz** for **WEBSTER 2026**. It helps students report campus concerns, track complaint progress, and receive updates through a transparent and accountable process.

Students can submit categorized complaints with priority levels and supporting attachments, with an option to hide their identity from the staff interface. Authorized officials manage complaints through defined stages: **Filed, Assigned, In Review, Resolved, and Closed**. Unresolved complaints are automatically escalated to higher authorities when their Service Level Agreement (SLA) deadlines expire.

The platform uses a **SHA-256 hash-chained audit trail** to make changes to recorded complaint history detectable. Role-based access controls protect complaint information, while notifications keep participants informed. A public dashboard displays aggregate resolution statistics without exposing complaint details.

Built using **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB**, the project aims to reduce delays, establish clear responsibility, and improve trust in institutional grievance handling.
